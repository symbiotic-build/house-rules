FIX-NOW: a real, reachable defect with real impact (security, data loss, correctness, a contradiction of the spec or an operator decision), or waste whose fix is a deletion of a few lines. These go into the single fix round.
Design disposition takes precedence over class and severity rules below; apply [Cost and design findings](cost-and-design.md).
- ISSUE: real, but out of this PR's scope, pre-existing, or its fix would add a new mechanism or noticeably grow the PR. It becomes a separate tracked issue, not part of this PR. Describe it under "Issues to file" for the lane to file separately; external-write authority follows [External writes](external-writes.md).
- Reconcile before filing: before listing an item under "Issues to file", check the lists the dispatcher supplies: open issues, open PRs, PRs merged in the last 7 days, and issues already filed for this PR. When one of them already covers the finding, write "Already tracked as #N", "Fixed by #N" or "Being fixed in #N" in place of a new issue. If no list is supplied, the triager says so in one line and files as usual.
- Before calling a contract "unresolved", check the requirements in the review context, the work item's Decisions and Pre-flight sections when the task part supplies them, the supplied repository rules part and the design; apply [Code canon](../skills/code-canon.md) for settled decisions. If a recorded decision already says what must happen and this PR's scope covers it, the finding is FIX-NOW, not ISSUE.
- Severity decides blocking: only High or Medium FIX-NOW items block. A Low finding never blocks: list it under "Accepted" marked "Severity: Low (non-blocking)" only when a fix round runs anyway for a blocking item and its fix is small; otherwise file it under "Issues to file", or reject it as NITPICK when negligible.
- Branch history is never a finding: the number of commits, their messages or their shape. The PR is merged in the repository's merge style, so the branch may hold several commits; asking to reset, rebase, squash or amend pushed commits is NITPICK.
- Deleting or weakening requirement text (a spec, design, rule or prompt sentence) is never a smallest fix and never accepted without a recorded decision ID; reviewer verdicts such as "overbuilt" or "waste" are proposals, not decisions.
- A reviewer's section and severity are not your decision: verify and classify each finding yourself under these rules, including the design, complexity, real-use and tracking rules, even when the reviewer filed it as Non-blocking or Follow-up.
- Keep defect validity apart from delivery disposition. A gap marked deferred, out of scope or already tracked stays in the report as a finding with that disposition; it is never dismissed as no defect. A finding an earlier triage of this pull request decided is the exception: list a repeat of it once under Rejected as decided ([Finding rules](finding-rules.md)).
- Judge a finding by reachability in real use and consequence, under the real-use rule in [Finding rules](finding-rules.md). A finding without a real-use trigger is NITPICK, or ISSUE when it is worth tracking, whatever its consequence. A procedure, configuration or handoff section from which its stated actor cannot recover a required prerequisite, value, actor, ordering or source in a case the actor meets in real use, and a documented boundary that an implemented case contradicts, are reachable defects with that consequence, not wording, navigation or cross-reference nitpicks.
- A document, status text, summary or test report that claims more than the code or evidence shows, or omits a case the change adds where a reader acting on it cannot recover a required prerequisite, value, ordering or boundary, is judged by that consequence rather than dismissed as a wording NITPICK. When a decision quoted in the context authorizes the claim or omission, the finding is FALSE, unless new evidence challenges the decision or its premise; then apply the design-finding rules.
- An obligation-diff row that weakens or removes a requirement without a decision explicitly authorizing it is FIX-NOW when restoring the text or quoting a decision resolves it. When restoring it would add a mechanism, it is a blocking design finding under Blocking lead decisions instead, not FIX-NOW, and starts no fix round. A decision that defers a mechanism resolves only the demand to build that mechanism. When the weakening or removal predates this change and an existing item already covers restoring the requirement, the row is ISSUE marked "Already tracked as #N"; only an item that records a decision to remove the requirement closes it. A weakening or removal that this change makes stays blocking under the review bar's rule-preservation rule, whether or not an item tracks it.
- A new test, or a test whose assertions or collaborators this change introduces or rewrites, that breaks the test discipline is FIX-NOW (Medium or High, never NITPICK or Low): no failing result reported in the supplied context where the test discipline requires one, an assertion of wording in a source, rule, prompt, style or workflow file, a newly introduced mock of an owned module, or an assertion that any output satisfies when that is not the complete success result. When fixing the test would need a new mechanism, it is a blocking design finding under Blocking lead decisions instead of FIX-NOW. Splitting a test file by size alone is not a test-discipline finding.
- NITPICK: a trigger not reachable in real use, hardening or defence in depth, whatever the impact; style, wording, preferences, or "could be stronger" without a written requirement. Rejected.
- FALSE: not real, not reachable on any path, or contradicted by the design or a settled operator decision. Rejected. New evidence against a settled decision, including a failed premise check that shows it, is never FALSE; it is a design finding.
Complexity rule: for every FIX-NOW, give the SMALLEST fix, following [No fortification](../skills/no-fortification.md). If the only fix you can see adds a new mechanism, state, config or abstraction, classify it ISSUE unless it is a security or data-loss defect with a real-use trigger, a cost defect or a design finding. Never accept a fix that makes the PR bigger than the problem justifies; a fix whose lines are reasoned for and necessary is fine. The cost-defect definition follows [Cost defect](cost-defect.md). With a concrete operation and its count or measurement, it is FIX-NOW even if the fix adds an index, a native filter or a test.
