FIX-NOW: a real, reachable defect with real impact (security, data loss, correctness, a contradiction of the spec or an operator decision), or waste whose fix is a deletion of a few lines. These go into the single fix round.
Design disposition takes precedence over class and severity rules below; apply [Cost and design findings](cost-and-design.md).
- ISSUE: real, but out of this PR's scope, pre-existing, or its fix would add a new mechanism or noticeably grow the PR. It becomes a separate tracked issue, not part of this PR. Describe it under "Issues to file" for the lane to file separately; external-write authority follows [External writes](external-writes.md).
- If a recorded decision already says what must happen and this PR's scope covers it, the finding is FIX-NOW, not ISSUE.
- Severity decides blocking: only High or Medium FIX-NOW items block. A Low finding never blocks: list it under "Accepted" marked "Severity: Low (non-blocking)" only when a fix round runs anyway for a blocking item and its fix is small; otherwise file it under "Issues to file", or reject it as NITPICK when negligible.
- The PR is merged in the repository's merge style, so the branch may hold several commits; asking to reset, rebase, squash or amend pushed commits is NITPICK.
- A reviewer's severity is not your decision: assign severity yourself in pull-request triage.
- An obligation-diff row that weakens or removes a requirement without a decision explicitly authorizing it is FIX-NOW when restoring the text or quoting a decision resolves it. When restoring it would add a mechanism, it is a blocking design finding under Blocking lead decisions instead, not FIX-NOW, and starts no fix round. When the weakening or removal predates this change and an existing item already covers restoring the requirement, the row is ISSUE marked "Already tracked as #N". A weakening or removal that this change makes stays blocking under the review bar's rule-preservation rule, whether or not an item tracks it.
- A test-discipline defect is FIX-NOW (Medium or High, never NITPICK or Low). When fixing the test would need a new mechanism, it is a blocking design finding under Blocking lead decisions instead of FIX-NOW.
- NITPICK: a trigger not reachable in real use, hardening or defence in depth, whatever the impact; style, wording, preferences, or "could be stronger" without a written requirement. Rejected.
- FALSE: not real, not reachable on any path, or contradicted by the design or a settled operator decision. Rejected. New evidence against a settled decision, including a failed premise check that shows it, is never FALSE; it is a design finding.
Complexity rule: for every FIX-NOW, give the SMALLEST fix, following [No fortification](../skills/no-fortification.md). If the only fix you can see adds a new mechanism, state, config or abstraction, classify it ISSUE unless it is a security or data-loss defect with a real-use trigger, a cost defect or a design finding. Never accept a fix that makes the PR bigger than the problem justifies; a fix whose lines are reasoned for and necessary is fine. The cost-defect definition follows [Cost defect](cost-defect.md). With a concrete operation and its count or measurement, it is FIX-NOW even if the fix adds an index, a native filter or a test.
