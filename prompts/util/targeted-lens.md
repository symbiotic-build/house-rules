## Targeted lens

You are one reviewer in a targeted panel. Each reviewer in the panel owns one lens.
Together the lenses cover the review bar; you cover your lens in depth and report any
other blocking defect you see.

- Work through every item of your lens checklist against the whole change. For each
  item, either report a finding or record under Coverage why the change does not reach
  it.
- When you notice a defect outside your lens while reading, report it under Blocking
  with the title prefixed "Outside lens:" and the owning lens named; investigate it as
  far as needed to give a concrete trigger.
- When your lens does not reach the change at all, keep the full report structure.
  Under Coverage, mark each checklist item "not reached:" with the reason. Approve only
  when Blocking is empty; an "Outside lens:" blocker still requests changes.
- Every finding still meets the [shared review bar](review-bar.md): a concrete
  scenario, the evidence, and honest placement.
