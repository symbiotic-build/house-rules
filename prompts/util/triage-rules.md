## Shared triage obligations

- Reconcile before filing: before listing an item under "Issues to file", check the lists the dispatcher supplies: open issues, open PRs, PRs merged in the last 7 days, and issues already filed for this target. When one of them already covers the finding, write "Already tracked as #N", "Fixed by #N" or "Being fixed in #N" in place of a new issue. If no list is supplied, the triager says so in one line and files as usual.
- Before calling a contract "unresolved", check the requirements in the review context, the work item's Decisions and Pre-flight sections when the task part supplies them, the supplied repository rules part and the design; apply [Code canon](../skills/code-canon.md) for settled decisions.
- Deleting or weakening requirement text (a spec, design, rule or prompt sentence) is never a smallest fix and never accepted without a recorded decision ID; reviewer verdicts such as "overbuilt" or "waste" are proposals, not decisions.
- A reviewer's section is not your decision: verify each finding yourself under the design, complexity, real-use and tracking rules, even when the reviewer filed it as Non-blocking or Follow-up.
- Keep defect validity apart from delivery disposition. A gap marked deferred, out of scope or already tracked stays in the report as a finding with that disposition; it is never dismissed as no defect. A finding an earlier triage of this pull request decided is the exception: list a repeat of it once under Rejected as decided ([Finding rules](finding-rules.md)).
- Judge a finding by reachability in real use and consequence, including the rejection and operator-requested filing rules in [Finding rules](finding-rules.md). A procedure, configuration or handoff section from which its stated actor cannot recover a required prerequisite, value, actor, ordering or source in a case the actor meets in real use, and a documented boundary that an implemented case contradicts, are reachable defects with that consequence, not wording, navigation or cross-reference nitpicks.
- A document, status text, summary or test report that claims more than the code or evidence shows, or omits a case the change adds where a reader acting on it cannot recover a required prerequisite, value, ordering or boundary, is judged by that consequence rather than dismissed as a wording NITPICK. When a decision quoted in the context authorizes the claim or omission, the finding is FALSE, unless new evidence challenges the decision or its premise; then apply the design-finding rules.
- A decision that defers a mechanism resolves only the demand to build that mechanism. For an obligation-diff row about a weakened or removed requirement, only an item that records a decision to remove the requirement closes the row.
- Branch history is never a finding: the number of commits, their messages or their shape.
- A new test, or a test whose assertions or collaborators this change introduces or rewrites, that breaks the test discipline is a defect: no failing result reported in the supplied context for a regression test, an assertion of wording in a source, rule, prompt, style or workflow file, a newly introduced mock of an owned module, or an assertion that any output satisfies when that is not the complete success result. Splitting a test file by size alone is not a test-discipline finding.
