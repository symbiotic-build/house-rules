# Registered entity

A registered entity lets readers resolve one technical meaning through a permanent identity and one Markdown home.

Several entities may share an [addressable section](sections.md#addressable-section).
Each still has a distinct home anchor and complete entry.
The home anchor immediately precedes its heading.
The first paragraph defines meaning.
The home includes nested declarations and examples through the next heading of equal or higher level.

The [entity index](entities.md#entity-index) owns identity, classification, accepted forms, rejected forms and home location.
The Markdown home owns meaning, facts and operational explanation.
The generated [complete registry](registry.md#complete-registry) combines both sources.
Authors never edit that generated combination.

Examples, diagrams and summaries illustrate obligations; they do not add obligations.
Excluded features remain in scope or non-goals text.
Exclusion alone creates no operational home or generated exclusion file.
The [home and linking requirement](requirements.md#req-homes) preserves one writable authority.

In the bounded-batch example, `example.batcher` resolves to the Batcher heading and its complete explanation.
The exact files are explained later in [Minimal corpus](example.md#minimal-example).
